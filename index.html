Inception (2010)

Rating: ★★★★☆

A mind-bending thriller by Christopher Nolan that explores dreams within dreams. Stunning visuals and a gripping plot make this a must-watch.

The Dark Knight (2008)

Rating: ★★★★★

One of the greatest superhero films ever made. Heath Ledger’s Joker performance is legendary, and the film balances action with deep themes.

Interstellar (2014)

Rating: ★★★★☆

A visually stunning space epic that explores love, time, and survival. Hans Zimmer’s score elevates the emotional depth of the film.
