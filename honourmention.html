Memento (2000)

Rating: ★★★★☆

A gripping psychological thriller about memory and revenge. Nolan’s non-linear storytelling keeps viewers questioning reality until the very end.

Dunkirk (2017)

Rating: ★★★★☆

A tense and immersive war film that depicts the evacuation of Dunkirk from land, sea, and air. Minimal dialogue, stunning visuals, and Hans Zimmer’s ticking score heighten the suspense.
